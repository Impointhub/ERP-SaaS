# 10.1 Create cash out

## 10.1.1: User redirect to login page

* Given I have not logged in
* When I type /finance/point/cash into browser
* Then I should be redirected to the login page

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/▶ COA Form Plan for CRUD UI.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 10.1.2 : User cannot see make a payment button

* Given I already logged in
* And I do not have permission to create cash out
* When I type `/finance/point/cash` into browser
* Then I should not see the "Make a Payment" button

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (721).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 10.1.3 : Display Notification Cash account is required

* Given I already logged in
* And I have permission to create cash out
* And I am on the page `/finance/point/cash`
* When I click "Make a Payment" button

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (722).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* And I select "PP/0002/VIII/26" from column payment order reference

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (724).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* And I click create button

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (725).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* And I leave column cash account empty

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (726).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* And I click submit

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (728).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Then I should see the message "Cash account is required"

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (729).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
